# Code of conduct

This is a small home-automation project. The rules are short because the
stakes are low and the courtesies are ordinary.

**Be decent.** Assume the person on the other end is doing their best with a
different intercom, a different network and less time than they would like.

**Not welcome:** personal attacks, harassment, demeaning comments about
someone's setup or skill level, and anything you would not say to someone
standing in your kitchen.

**Remember what this project is.** Nobody here is paid, and nobody owes you a
fix, a review or a release. "This is broken, fix it" gets much less than
"here is what I ran, here is what happened, here is what I expected".

**Scope.** This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and commit
messages in this repository.

**Enforcement.** The maintainer may edit, lock, or delete comments and block
accounts, at their discretion and without a process. Report a problem by
opening an issue, or privately through GitHub if that is not appropriate.
